Research

Research Focus

My research explores computer architecture, energy-efficient computing, and autonomous systems. I am especially interested in hardware-software co-design for real-time perception and in understanding how emerging workloads should shape future computing platforms.

Computer Architecture Architectural support for emerging, data-intensive workloads.
Sustainable Computing Energy-aware systems and performance-emission tradeoffs.
Autonomous Systems Efficient real-time perception through hardware-software co-design.

Current Research

Primary project

Accelerating Point Cloud-Based 3D Perception

I am investigating efficient execution of 3D perception workloads for autonomous vehicles, with an emphasis on object detection and segmentation.

  • Profile state-of-the-art 3D object detection and segmentation models.
  • Identify compute and memory bottlenecks on NVIDIA GPUs.
  • Explore hardware-software co-design opportunities for lower-latency perception.
Ongoing

UCIe for Autonomous Vehicle Systems

I am simulating and testing the Universal Chiplet Interconnect Express protocol to study chiplet-based system integration for autonomous vehicle workloads.
